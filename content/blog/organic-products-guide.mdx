At Velvet Studio, every product that touches your hair or skin is organic and cruelty-free. Here is why that matters.

## The Problem With Conventional Products

Most salon products contain chemicals like ammonia, parabens, sulfates, and formaldehyde. These provide short-term results but cause long-term damage — dryness, breakage, scalp irritation, and even allergic reactions.

## What Organic Means

Organic salon products are formulated with naturally derived ingredients. They are free from harsh chemicals and synthetic fragrances. Key benefits include:

- **Gentler on sensitive skin** — Fewer irritants mean fewer reactions
- **Better long-term hair health** — Natural oils nourish rather than strip
- **Environmentally responsible** — Biodegradable formulas, sustainable sourcing
- **No animal testing** — Cruelty-free certification on every product we stock

## Our Product Partners

We work with certified organic brands that share our values. Each product is vetted for ingredient transparency and efficacy before it enters our salon.

## The Results Speak

Clients who switch to organic treatments consistently report softer hair, healthier scalp, and longer-lasting color. The transition period is brief, and the benefits are permanent.
