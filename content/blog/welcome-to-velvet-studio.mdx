We are thrilled to introduce Velvet Studio to Indiranagar. Born from a passion for clean beauty and expert craftsmanship, our boutique salon is designed to be your sanctuary in the city.

## Why We Started

After training in London and working with some of the best stylists in the world, I realized that Bangalore deserved a salon that prioritized both quality and consciousness. Every product we use is organic, cruelty-free, and sourced with care.

## What Makes Us Different

At Velvet Studio, we believe beauty should never come at the cost of health. Our approach is simple:

- **Organic Products Only** — No ammonia, no parabens, no compromise
- **Personalized Consultations** — Every service begins with understanding you
- **Relaxing Environment** — Designed to make you feel at home

## Visit Us

We are located on 12th Main, HAL 2nd Stage, Indiranagar. Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking ahead on weekends.

Come experience the Velvet difference.
