Your wedding day is one of the most photographed days of your life. Here are our top tips to ensure you look and feel your absolute best.

## 1. Start Your Skincare Routine Early

Begin a dedicated skincare routine at least 3 months before the wedding. Consistency with cleansing, moisturizing, and sunscreen will give you that natural glow no amount of makeup can replicate.

## 2. Do a Trial Run

Always schedule a trial session for your hair and makeup. This lets you experiment with looks, adjust based on your outfit, and eliminates day-of stress.

## 3. Hydrate From Within

Drink at least 3 liters of water daily in the weeks leading up to your wedding. Hydrated skin holds makeup better and photographs beautifully.

## 4. Choose Long-Lasting Formulas

Opt for waterproof mascara, long-wear foundation, and setting sprays. Your celebration will last hours — your makeup should too.

## 5. Plan Your Timeline

Work backwards from your ceremony time. Professional bridal makeup typically takes 2-3 hours including hair styling. Give yourself buffer time to enjoy the process.
